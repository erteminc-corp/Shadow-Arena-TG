# Art & audio

Original assets generated for this project with the built-in image generation tool. No stock sprite libraries, borrowed game characters or third-party image URLs are used. Images are bundled locally.

## Arena prompt

Production game environment texture, portrait 2:3 aspect ratio. Entire image is an orthographic overhead dark fantasy ritual arena, no perspective horizon, no text, no characters, no UI. Premium hand-painted detailed graphite slate flagstones, cracks, large central subtle silver rune circle on open flat playable floor, sparse rubble and broken columns strictly along outside edges, small blue flame braziers at four corners, muted crimson and violet accents, intricate weathered stone material, atmospheric cool blue light, center readable mid-dark desaturated stone rather than black. Arena bounded by rectangular ancient stone rim. Playable central 85 percent completely unobstructed. Sophisticated original indie game illustration.

Saved project asset: arena.webp (optimized from the generated PNG).

## Character atlas prompt

A production 2D game character sprite atlas on truly transparent background. Wide landscape image with five equally spaced characters in one horizontal row, each character isolated entirely inside its own equal-width column, full body feet visible, same ground line. No text labels, ground or shadows. Premium hand-painted dark fantasy characters, crisp detailed silhouettes readable small. All face forward slightly 3/4. Column 1: Ash Warden, black hood, narrow silver mask, asymmetrical engraved silver armor, deep crimson torn cloak, slim warrior, silver mystical sword pointing down, faint cyan magic hand. Column 2: Gloomling, small wiry hunched shadow beast with long claws, blue white eyes, violet-black skin. Column 3: Iron Husk, huge heavy rusted iron armored construct with amber light between elaborate plates. Column 4: Void Archer, tall spectral violet hooded archer, thin ornate curved bow, floating torn violet robes, cyan eyes. Column 5: Rift Herald, huge menacing broken silver armored horned mystic creature, bright purple glowing chest core, jagged armor, black torn robes. Each design unique and intricate, coherent painted materials and cool rim lighting. No copies of existing game characters. Equal column layout with generous separation and all body parts within column.

The generated atlas was separated into five transparent project PNGs: warden.png, gloom.png, husk.png, archer.png, herald.png. Runtime clipping divides the art into independently animated regions. Sword/staff overlays, card icons, lighting, attack arcs and particles are original Canvas/SVG code. The original generated poses are not directional sprite sheets; horizontal facing uses mirroring.

## Sound

blade.wav, fire.wav, dash.wav, hit.wav, kill.wav, wave.wav, menu.wav, death.wav are short original PCM effects synthesized from envelopes, oscillators and seeded noise. No external samples or music dependencies. 22,050 Hz mono WAV, decoded once into reusable Web Audio buffers.
